FAQ

Kashmir tour questions, answered

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

Kashmir is beautiful all year. March to June is ideal for gardens, tulips and pleasant weather; July to September for green meadows; October and November for golden autumn colours; and December to February for snow and skiing in Gulmarg. Tell us what you want to see and we will suggest the right month.

Is Kashmir safe for tourists and families?

Yes. The main tourist areas of Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonmarg welcome lakhs of tourists every year. We use verified hotels and experienced local drivers, and our team stays in touch with you throughout the trip. We also keep you updated on weather and road conditions.

What is included in your tour packages?

Our packages usually include hotel or houseboat stays, breakfast and dinner, a private cab for all transfers and sightseeing, airport or station pickup and drop, tolls, parking and driver allowance. Each package card shows its exact inclusions and exclusions under “View itinerary”.

How do I book a tour package from Agra?

Send us a WhatsApp message or fill in the enquiry form with your travel dates and number of travellers. We will share a customised itinerary and quote, usually within a few hours. Once you are happy with the plan, a small advance confirms your booking.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and cash at our Sikandra office. A part payment confirms the booking and the balance is paid before the trip starts. You get a proper receipt for every payment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made well before the travel date get a refund after deducting non-refundable hotel and booking charges. Closer to the date, charges depend on hotel policies. Dates can often be moved instead of cancelling. The exact terms are shared in writing with your quote.
